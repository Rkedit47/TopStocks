The Business

What Does ZenaTech, Inc. Actually Do?

ZenaTech, Inc. is an AI drone technology company operating a Drone-as-a-Service platform, an enterprise software business, and the ZenaDrone hardware program spanning commercial and defense applications.

What they sell or develop

The company acquires established surveying, inspection, and field-service businesses and plans to retrofit them with drones and automation, sells and services enterprise SaaS products across multiple industries, and develops the ZenaDrone 1000, IQ Nano, IQ Square, IQ Quad, IQ Aqua, IQ Sphere, ZenaDrone 2000, and Interceptor P-1 platforms.

Who their customers are

Customers include enterprise field-service clients across surveying, engineering, environmental, oil-and-gas, agriculture, and inspection markets, longstanding SaaS customers of the acquired software businesses, and government and defense buyers now beginning U.S. agency demonstrations.

The problem they solve

Field-service operators face acute labor shortages and rising costs for manual survey, inspection, and monitoring workflows, while defense and public-safety agencies need NDAA-compliant, AI-enabled aerial systems that can operate in GPS-denied and contested environments.

How they generate revenue

Revenue comes from Drone-as-a-Service subscriptions and per-job fees, drone hardware sales, enterprise SaaS licenses and maintenance, professional services, and — over time — targeted defense procurement following certification.

Where they operate

Headquartered in Toronto, Canada, with three drone manufacturing facilities (UAE, Arizona, Taiwan), operations across North America, Europe, the UAE, and Asia, and newly established Ukraine operations focused on Counter-UAS manufacturing and testing.

Current stage of development

ZenaTech is a revenue-generating, dual-listed operator (NASDAQ: ZENA, FSE: 49Q). FY2025 revenue reached C$12.9M (+558% YoY); Q1 2026 revenue reached C$8.4M (+640% YoY) with ~93% from DaaS. The company has completed 25 DaaS acquisitions to date and is not yet profitable — Q1 2026 net loss was C$26.5M with C$18.8M of operating cash used.

The Opportunity

Why Could ZENA Be Interesting?

The points below draw on the company's public disclosures, its recent operational updates, and TopStocks commentary. We distinguish between confirmed facts, company statements, and our own editorial framing.

01

A Three-Layer Business, Not Just A Drone Company

ZenaTech operates three connected layers: a Drone-as-a-Service roll-up that acquires surveying and inspection companies (generating the vast majority of current revenue), an enterprise-software portfolio of recurring-license products, and the ZenaDrone hardware program covering commercial, indoor, underwater, and defense platforms. The DaaS layer produces revenue today; the defense layer is the upside story.

02

Explosive Revenue Growth

FY2025 revenue grew ~558% to C$12.9M, with C$10.1M (78%) from Drone-as-a-Service. Q1 2026 revenue grew another ~640% year-over-year to C$8.4M, with DaaS accounting for ~93% of the quarter. Annualizing Q1 (not official guidance) implies a C$33.6M run rate — a very different scale of business than one year ago.

03

25 Acquisitions Complete, More Under Offer

ZenaTech completed its 25th DaaS acquisition — Velocity Geomatics — on July 16, 2026. Two days earlier, the company signed offers on additional surveying and geospatial businesses across the U.S., Canada, and Australia that management estimates could contribute approximately C$40M of revenue in the first 12 months after closing. Those deals have not yet closed and the estimate is based on unaudited target-company information.

04

First Confirmed U.S. Defense-Agency Demonstration

On July 21, 2026, ZenaDrone announced it is beginning to schedule demonstrations with U.S. government defense agencies, starting with its first confirmed agency engagement. The IQ Nano will be demonstrated first, with additional platforms following. A demonstration is a pathway toward a possible contract — not a contract itself — but it is a concrete step from internal development toward government procurement.

05

Blue UAS Certification In Progress

The ZenaDrone 1000, IQ Square, and IQ Nano advanced into the cybersecurity phase of the Green-to-Blue UAS certification framework in May 2026. Successful completion could remove a major barrier between technology demonstrations and approved U.S. government procurement channels, though certification is not guaranteed on any specific timeline.

06

Expanding Product Portfolio Across Commercial And Defense

The ZenaDrone family spans the flagship ZenaDrone 1000 (surveillance, inspection, cargo — expected $100K–$250K price plus software), the IQ Series (Nano, Square, Quad, Aqua) for enterprise and indoor use, the newly prototyped IQ Sphere for GPS-denied and confined-space operations, and the ZenaDrone 2000 and Interceptor P-1 Counter-UAS platforms — supported by manufacturing in the UAE, Arizona, and Taiwan.

The Flagship Product

Meet ZenaTech's Flagship Drone

The ZenaDrone 1000 is ZenaTech's flagship autonomous drone, built for demanding commercial, industrial, and defense missions.

Its heavy-lift design, long-range capabilities, and advanced sensors are intended for applications including surveillance, inspection, mapping, agriculture, cargo transport, and emergency response.

According to the company, the platform has completed paid trials involving the U.S. Air Force and Navy and is expected to sell for approximately $100,000 to $250,000 plus software.

Paid trials and demonstrations do not guarantee future government contracts or material revenue.

Why It Matters for Investors

  • High-value hardware could generate meaningful revenue per unit.

  • Software, maintenance, and customization may create additional recurring income.

  • Defense testing could provide a pathway toward pilot programs and future contracts.

  • The platform gives ZenaTech a recognizable flagship product beyond its acquisition-driven service business.
