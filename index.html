The Business

What Does ZenaTech, Inc. Actually Do?

ZenaTech, Inc. is an AI drone technology company operating a Drone-as-a-Service platform, an enterprise software business, and the ZenaDrone hardware program spanning commercial and defense applications.

What they sell or develop

The company acquires established surveying, inspection, and field-service businesses and plans to retrofit them with drones and automation, sells and services enterprise SaaS products across multiple industries, and develops the ZenaDrone 1000, IQ Nano, IQ Square, IQ Quad, IQ Aqua, IQ Sphere, ZenaDrone 2000, and Interceptor P-1 platforms.

Who their customers are

Customers include enterprise field-service clients across surveying, engineering, environmental, oil-and-gas, agriculture, and inspection markets, longstanding SaaS customers of the acquired software businesses, and government and defense buyers now beginning U.S. agency demonstrations.

The problem they solve

Field-service operators face acute labor shortages and rising costs for manual survey, inspection, and monitoring workflows, while defense and public-safety agencies need NDAA-compliant, AI-enabled aerial systems that can operate in GPS-denied and contested environments.

How they generate revenue

Revenue comes from Drone-as-a-Service subscriptions and per-job fees, drone hardware sales, enterprise SaaS licenses and maintenance, professional services, and — over time — targeted defense procurement following certification.

Where they operate

Headquartered in Toronto, Canada, with three drone manufacturing facilities (UAE, Arizona, Taiwan), operations across North America, Europe, the UAE, and Asia, and newly established Ukraine operations focused on Counter-UAS manufacturing and testing.

Current stage of development

ZenaTech is a revenue-generating, dual-listed operator (NASDAQ: ZENA, FSE: 49Q). FY2025 revenue reached C$12.9M (+558% YoY); Q1 2026 revenue reached C$8.4M (+640% YoY) with ~93% from DaaS. The company has completed 25 DaaS acquisitions to date and is not yet profitable — Q1 2026 net loss was C$26.5M with C$18.8M of operating cash used.
